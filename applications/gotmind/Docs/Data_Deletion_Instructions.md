# Data Deletion Instructions

**App Name:** Concentration Game
**Developer:** developer@ZoeWave.com

At ZoeWave, we prioritize your privacy through a "Local-First" architecture. We do not maintain user accounts or central servers. Below are the steps and information regarding the deletion of your data.

## 1. How to Delete Your Data

### Local Game Data (Progress, Scores, Settings)
All gameplay data is stored exclusively on your device.
* **Action:** To permanently delete all local data, simply **uninstall the app** from your device.
* **Result:** This action immediately removes all local records, and since we do not host this data on any server, it cannot be recovered.

### Analytics and Stability Data (Firebase)
The app uses Google Firebase to monitor stability (Crashlytics) and anonymized usage trends (Google Analytics). This data is not tied to a personal account, but is associated with an installation identifier.

* **Action:** If you wish to request the manual deletion of the anonymized analytics data associated with your device, please contact us at **[Insert Support Email]** and provide your **Firebase Device ID**.
* **Finding your ID:** You can find your unique Firebase Device ID within the app by navigating to **Settings > About**.
* **Result:** Upon receiving your ID, we will initiate a deletion request through the Firebase console to remove all records associated with that identifier.

## 2. Types of Data Deleted or Kept

* **Deleted:** All local game progress, high scores, custom settings, and (upon request) anonymized analytics events linked to your Device ID.
* **Kept:** We do not "keep" any personal data as we do not collect names, emails, or contact information. Only aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics (e.g., "Total number of games played by all users") remain in our records for quality improvement.

## 3. Data Retention Period

* **Local Data:** Remains on your device until the app is uninstalled.
* **Analytics Data:** Data sent to Firebase is automatically deleted after the standard retention period defined by Google (typically 14 months for analytics), unless a manual deletion is requested via the steps above.
