## Full Description
**Master your memory with gravity in MemBlox!**

GotMind presents **MemBlox**, a high-octane reimagining of the classic concentration game. Experience a high-performance arcade world where emojis fall from the heavens, gravity is your only constant, and your memory is the ultimate weapon.

### 🎮 DYNAMIC GAMEPLAY
Forget static grids. In MemBlox, blocks descend smoothly into the arena, giving you a fleeting window to memorize their identity before they flip and hide. Master the flow of "Zen Descent" or test your reflexes in high-speed modes.

### ⚡ OVERCLOCKED FRENZY
Chain together perfect matches to trigger **FRENZY MODE**. Watch as the arena pulses with neon energy, spawning speed hits overdrive, and every point you earn is **DOUBLED**. Can you handle the heat?

### 🛠️ STRATEGIC POWER-UPS
Turn the tide with a suite of advanced tactical tools:
*   **❄️ Freeze:** Stop time and halt all block movement.
*   **☢️ Nuke:** Clear the board with a high-impact physical shockwave.
*   **🔍 Scan:** Radar-ping the board to sequentially reveal all hidden matches.
*   **💎 Equalizer:** Instant-clear all blocks of a single emoji type.
*   **⏳ Slow:** Dilate time to 50% speed for perfect precision.
*   **🤖 Auto-Match:** Let the AI find a pair for you when the pressure peaks.

### 🏆 ELITE PROGRESSION
Rise through the ranks in our persistent **Hall of Fame**. Earn specialized merit medals for your skills:
*   🎯 **Sniper:** For those with elite match accuracy (>90%).
*   🔥 **Streak:** For hitting massive consecutive match combos.
*   🛡️ **Pro:** For true legends who win without using power-ups.

### ✨ 7-STAR POLISH
MemBlox is designed for total immersion:
*   🌌 **Immersive HUD:** An ultra-slim "Heads-Up Display" that maximizes your game area.
*   💫 **Cinematic VFX:** 3D card flips, particle confetti bursts, and physical screen-shake.
*   🌌 **Galactic Background:** A dynamic nebula of floating stellar dust that reacts to game speed.
*   📳 **Haptic Feedback:** Feel every match, explosion, and nuke with integrated haptic engineering.
