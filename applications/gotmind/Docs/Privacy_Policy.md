# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our Concentration Game (the "App"). We are committed to a "Local-First" and privacy-centric approach to mobile gaming.

## 1. Core Principles
* **Free Forever:** The App is provided at no cost.
* **Ad-Free:** There are no advertisements within the App.
* **No In-App Purchases:** There are no financial transactions or "pay-to-win" mechanics.
* **No Accounts:** We do not require you to create an account or provide an email address to play.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal information (such as your name, address, or contacts). The App is designed to function entirely without a central server.

To ensure a stable and high-quality user experience, the app utilizes industry-standard third-party Service Providers that may collect information used to identify the device for stability and performance metrics:

* **Google Play Services:** Used for app distribution and core functionality.
* **Firebase Crashlytics:** Used to monitor app stability and report technical bugs/crashes.
* **Google Analytics for Firebase:** Used to collect anonymized usage metrics (e.g., which levels are played most) to help us improve the game design.

## 3. Data Storage and Internet Usage
The App operates using **Local Storage Only**. 
* All game progress, scores, and settings are stored directly on your device. 
* We do not maintain any servers and never transmit your gameplay data over the internet.
* Internet access is utilized solely by the third-party SDKs mentioned above for anonymized stability and performance reporting.

## 4. Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device, we have no access to your data and cannot delete it for you. 
* **To delete all data:** Simply uninstall the App from your device. This will permanently remove all local files, progress, and settings associated with the App.

## 5. Source Availability
The source code for this App is available for review ("Source Available"), though it is not licensed as Open Source. This transparency is intended to verify our privacy claims and technical architecture.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Since the App collects no personal data, it is safe for all ages; however, we encourage parents to review the privacy policies of the third-party services (Google/Firebase) listed above.

## 7. Security
We value your trust in using our App. Because the App uses local storage and does not transmit personal data to us, the primary security of your data rests on the security of your own device.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or on our store listing.
