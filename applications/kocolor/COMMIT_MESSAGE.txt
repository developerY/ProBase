Fix Health Connect Permissions per Google Play Policy Review

This commit addresses the "Excessive data access for declared feature" violation flagged during Google Play's policy review regarding the Health Connect "Minimum Scope" requirements.

Summary of Changes:
1. **Manifest Stripping**: Actively removed `READ_DISTANCE`, `READ_EXERCISE`, `READ_HEART_RATE`, `READ_NUTRITION`, and `READ_WEIGHT` declarations from `AndroidManifest.xml` via `tools:node="remove"`.
2. **Runtime Exclusions**: Purged the corresponding permission constants from the `permissions` Set across `HealthViewModel.kt` and `GetHealthSummaryUseCase.kt` to ensure the application no longer prompts the user for these unjustified metrics during the Health Connect onboarding flow.
3. **Calorie Fix**: Reinstated the `READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED` permission into the core Kotlin permission arrays to maintain parity with the manifest declarations, supporting existing baseline activity calculations.

Status: Play Store compliant. Ready for immediate resubmission.
