# Data Deletion Instructions for KoColor

At **KoColor**, we prioritize your privacy and ensure you have full control over your data. Because KoColor is designed with a **Zero-Footprint** mandate, your information is stored exclusively on your local device.

## 1. Automated Data Deletion
The simplest and most complete way to delete all data associated with KoColor is to uninstall the application from your device.

*   **Android**: Long-press the KoColor icon on your home screen or app drawer, select **"Uninstall"** or the trash can icon, and confirm.
*   **Result**: This action permanently deletes your local Room database, which includes your style profiles, analysis history, saved color palettes, and beauty routines.

## 2. Manual In-App Deletion
If you wish to delete specific pieces of data without uninstalling the app, you can do so directly within the settings or feature screens:

*   **Analysis History**: Navigate to the **Color** tab and use the "Discard" or "Delete" options on individual analysis cards.
*   **Vault Inventories**: Navigate to the **Vault** and remove individual items (Face, Hair, Clothes, Shoes).
*   **Beauty Routines**: Individual routine steps can be cleared or reset within the **Routines** feature.

## 3. Remote Data (BYOK Model)
KoColor does not store your data on any central servers. However, since KoColor utilizes a **Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)** model for Gemini AI:

*   **API Interactions**: Any visual or text data sent to the Gemini API is subject to Google's data handling policies.
*   **Google AI Studio**: You can manage your API key and review any usage logs directly through your [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) dashboard.

## 4. Contact for Privacy Inquiries
If you have questions regarding your data or our privacy architecture, please contact us at: **Developer@ZoeWave.com**.
