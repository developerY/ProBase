# Makeup Categories & Subcategories Explained

## Face Makeup

### Primer

Products applied before foundation to improve makeup wear, smooth texture, and control skin concerns.

* **Silicone-based primer** — Creates a smooth surface and fills fine lines/pores.
* **Hydrating primer** — Adds moisture for dry or dehydrated skin.
* **Mattifying primer** — Controls oil and reduces shine.
* **Pore-filling primer** — Blurs enlarged pores for smoother makeup.
* **Illuminating primer** — Adds glow and radiance under makeup.
* **Color-correcting primer** — Neutralizes discoloration using color theory.
* **Grip/tacky primer** — Helps makeup adhere longer.
* **SPF primer** — Combines makeup prep with sun protection.

---

### Foundation

Creates an even skin tone and base complexion.

* **Liquid foundation** — Most common; available in many finishes and coverage levels.
* **Powder foundation** — Lightweight and oil-controlling.
* **Cream foundation** — Richer formula with fuller coverage.
* **Stick foundation** — Portable solid formula for quick application.
* **Serum foundation** — Lightweight with skincare ingredients.
* **Cushion foundation** — Liquid foundation in a sponge compact, popular in K-beauty.
* **Mineral foundation** — Powder-based with mineral ingredients, often gentler on skin.
* **Airbrush foundation** — Spray-applied for ultra-smooth professional finish.
* **Tinted moisturizer** — Light coverage with hydration.
* **BB cream** — “Beauty balm” combining skincare and light makeup.
* **CC cream** — “Color correcting” cream focused on evening skin tone.

---

### Concealer

Used to hide blemishes, dark circles, redness, or discoloration.

* **Liquid concealer** — Flexible and versatile for most skin types.
* **Cream concealer** — Thicker and fuller coverage.
* **Stick concealer** — Dense formula for spot concealing.
* **Corrective concealer** — Uses colors to neutralize skin issues.
* **Brightening concealer** — Lightens under-eye darkness.
* **Full-coverage concealer** — Covers severe discoloration or acne.

---

### Setting Products

Help makeup stay in place and reduce transfer or shine.

* **Loose powder** — Fine powder used to set liquid products.
* **Pressed powder** — Compact powder for portability and touch-ups.
* **Baking powder** — Used heavily under eyes or contours for long wear.
* **Blotting powder** — Reduces oil without adding heavy coverage.
* **Setting spray** — Locks makeup into place.
* **Fixing spray** — Stronger hold for long events or performances.
* **Finishing mist** — Melds makeup layers for a natural finish.

---

### Contour & Sculpting

Adds shadows to define facial structure.

* **Powder contour** — Easy-to-blend dry contour.
* **Cream contour** — More natural and skin-like finish.
* **Stick contour** — Convenient cream contour in stick form.
* **Bronzing contour** — Adds warmth while sculpting.
* **Cool-tone contour** — Mimics natural facial shadows.
* **Sculpting palettes** — Multi-shade contour kits.

---

### Bronzer

Adds warmth and sun-kissed color to the skin.

* **Matte bronzer** — Natural warmth without sparkle.
* **Shimmer bronzer** — Adds glow and luminosity.
* **Cream bronzer** — Dewy and blendable finish.
* **Liquid bronzer** — Lightweight radiant warmth.
* **Tanning drops** — Mixed into skincare for gradual bronze tone.

---

### Blush

Adds color and life to the cheeks.

* **Powder blush** — Traditional and long-lasting.
* **Cream blush** — Soft natural finish.
* **Liquid blush** — Lightweight with strong pigmentation.
* **Gel blush** — Sheer watery texture.
* **Tint blush** — Stains skin for long-lasting color.
* **Blush sticks** — Portable cream formula.

---

### Highlighter

Enhances areas where light naturally hits the face.

* **Powder highlighter** — Strong reflective glow.
* **Cream highlighter** — Natural luminous finish.
* **Liquid highlighter** — Mixable glow product.
* **Balm highlighter** — Dewy wet-look finish.
* **Glitter highlighter** — Sparkly dramatic shine.
* **Illuminating drops** — Concentrated glow additives.

---

### Color Correction

Neutralizes unwanted skin tones using opposite colors.

* **Green corrector** — Cancels redness.
* **Peach corrector** — Brightens mild dark circles.
* **Orange corrector** — Corrects deep dark pigmentation.
* **Purple corrector** — Brightens dull/yellow skin.
* **Yellow corrector** — Neutralizes purple tones.
* **Blue corrector** — Reduces overly orange tones.

---

# Eye Makeup

## Eyeshadow

Adds color, depth, and dimension to eyelids.

* **Powder eyeshadow** — Standard pressed or loose powder formula.
* **Cream eyeshadow** — Smooth creamy finish.
* **Liquid eyeshadow** — Fluid formula, often metallic or glittery.
* **Stick eyeshadow** — Crayon-style easy application.
* **Glitter eyeshadow** — Sparkling dramatic effect.
* **Metallic eyeshadow** — Reflective shimmer finish.
* **Matte eyeshadow** — Non-shiny smooth color.
* **Duo/multichrome shadows** — Color-shifting pigments.
* **Pigments** — Loose concentrated color powders.
* **Eyeshadow palettes** — Collections of coordinated shades.

---

## Eyeliner

Defines the eyes and lash line.

* **Pencil eyeliner** — Easy beginner-friendly liner.
* **Gel eyeliner** — Smooth intense pigment.
* **Liquid eyeliner** — Sharp dramatic lines.
* **Felt-tip eyeliner** — Marker-style precision liner.
* **Cake eyeliner** — Water-activated traditional liner.
* **Kohl liner** — Soft smoky eye pencil.
* **Tightlining liner** — Designed for waterline application.

---

## Mascara

Enhances eyelashes by adding length, curl, or volume.

* **Lengthening mascara** — Makes lashes appear longer.
* **Volumizing mascara** — Thickens lashes.
* **Curling mascara** — Helps lashes hold curl.
* **Waterproof mascara** — Resists water and sweat.
* **Tubing mascara** — Forms tubes around lashes for smudge resistance.
* **Lash primer** — Base coat before mascara.
* **Colored mascara** — Adds non-black lash color.

---

## Eyebrow Products

Shapes, fills, and defines brows.

* **Brow pencil** — Mimics hair strokes.
* **Brow pomade** — Thick cream for bold brows.
* **Brow powder** — Soft natural brow filling.
* **Brow gel** — Holds brow hairs in place.
* **Brow mascara** — Adds color and volume.
* **Brow pen** — Ultra-fine realistic strokes.
* **Brow wax** — Strong brow hold.
* **Brow tint** — Temporarily stains brows.

---

## False Lashes

Artificial lashes to enhance eye appearance.

* **Strip lashes** — Full lash strips applied to eyelid.
* **Individual lashes** — Single lash clusters for customization.
* **Magnetic lashes** — Attach using magnets instead of glue.
* **Cluster lashes** — Small grouped lash sections.
* **Lash glue** — Adhesive for false lashes.
* **Lash applicators** — Tools for easier lash placement.

---

* **Matte lipstick** — Flat non-shiny finish.
* **Satin lipstick** — Soft semi-shiny finish.
* **Cream lipstick** — Moisturizing rich texture.
* **Sheer lipstick** — Lightweight translucent color.
* **Metallic lipstick** — Reflective shimmer finish.
* **Velvet lipstick** — Soft blurred matte appearance.

---

* **Liquid lipstick** — Long-wear pigmented liquid formula.
* **Lip stain** — Tints lips for lasting color.
* **Lip tint** — Lightweight natural lip color.
* **Lip mousse** — Airy whipped texture.

---

## Gloss & Shine

* **Lip gloss** — Shiny reflective lip finish.
* **Plumping gloss** — Creates fuller lip appearance.
* **Shimmer gloss** — Gloss with sparkle particles.
* **Lip oil** — Hydrating glossy treatment.

---

## Lip Care & Prep

* **Lip balm** — Moisturizes dry lips.

* **Lip mask** — Intensive overnight treatment.

# Makeup Finish Types

* **Matte** — Flat shine-free finish.
* **Dewy** — Moist radiant glow.
* **Satin** — Between matte and glossy.
* **Natural** — Mimics real skin appearance.
* **Velvet** — Soft blurred finish.
* **Radiant** — Bright healthy glow.
* **Glitter** — Visible sparkle particles.
* **Metallic** — Reflective metal-like shine.

---

# Makeup Coverage Levels

* **Sheer** — Very light translucent coverage.
* **Light** — Slight evening of skin tone.
* **Medium** — Balanced visible coverage.
* **Full coverage** — Hides most imperfections completely.
* **Buildable coverage** — Can layer from light to full.

---

# Makeup Formula Types

* **Powder** — Dry pressed or loose formula.
* **Cream** — Thick moisturizing texture.
* **Liquid** — Fluid easy-blending texture.
* **Gel** — Jelly-like lightweight consistency.
* **Balm** — Waxy nourishing texture.
* **Stick** — Solid portable form.
* **Mousse** — Airy whipped consistency.
* **Serum** — Lightweight skincare-infused formula.
