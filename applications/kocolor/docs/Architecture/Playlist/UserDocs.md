# How to Use Your KoColor Style Playlist

Welcome to your Personal Style Operating System! The **Style Playlist** is the heart of KoColor. Instead of staring at your closet every morning, KoColor acts like your personal stylist, building a 7-day wardrobe plan based on your calendar, local weather, and unique color profile.

Here is how to get the most out of your weekly Style Playlist.

## 1. The Sunday Preview: Plan Your Week

Every weekend, KoColor's engine looks ahead at your upcoming week. It checks the weather forecast and scans your calendar events to build your next 7-Day Playlist.

* **Review the Forecast:** Open the Playlist tab to see a day-by-day breakdown of your outfits.
* **Check the "Why":** Tap on any outfit to see exactly *why* KoColor chose it (e.g., *"Linen blend for the 85° afternoon,"* or *"Navy perfectly harmonizes with your Soft Summer contrast."*)
* **Pin & Tweak:** Love Tuesday's dress but want to swap the shoes? Make your adjustments and tap **Pin**.
* **Accept the Week:** Once you are happy with the lineup, tap **Lock Playlist** to set your week in motion.

## 2. Your Daily Route: From Day to Night

KoColor knows that your day rarely stops at 5:00 PM. Your daily plan isn't just one outfit—it's a route.

* **The Base Outfit:** This is your primary look for the day, tailored to your main event (like a day at the office or running errands).
* **The Evening Remix:** If you have a dinner or event on your calendar, KoColor will suggest a "Remix"—a quick swap of a jacket, shoes, or accessory to effortlessly transition your base outfit into the evening.

## 3. The Cosmetic Crossfade

Sometimes, you just really want to wear a shirt that isn't technically in your perfect color season. KoColor won't stop you—it will adapt!

If your chosen outfit clashes with your natural color profile, look for the **Cosmetic Crossfade** section on your daily plan. KoColor will automatically dive into your digital makeup vanity and recommend specific shades (like a warm peach blush or a cool-toned lipstick) to visually bridge the gap, ensuring your whole look stays flawlessly cohesive.

## 4. Wear & Commit (The Most Important Step!)

KoColor is smart, but it only gets smarter if you tell it what you actually wore.

When you get dressed, always remember to tap the **"I'm Wearing This"** button on your daily plan.

* **Why does this matter?** Tapping this button logs the outfit into your Closet History. It immediately puts those garments on a 48-hour "cooldown," guaranteeing KoColor won't accidentally suggest the same pants again tomorrow.
* **Skipping a day?** If you end up staying in pajamas all day, just tap **Skip**. KoColor will adjust its math and keep those unworn clothes fresh for later in the week.

### 💡 Pro-Tips for the Best Playlists

* **Keep your Calendar synced:** The more KoColor knows about where you are going, the better it can dress you.
* **Upload your favorites:** The engine prioritizes items it knows you love, mixing them perfectly with items you haven't worn in a while to keep your rotation fresh.
* **Watch for "Style Wrapped":** Keep committing your daily wears! Over time, KoColor will generate insights showing you your most-worn "Hero Items" and your shifting style eras.
