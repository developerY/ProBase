# KoColor Rotation Screens Overview (V1)

This document outlines the purpose and content of the four primary screens in the KoColor Rotation system, designed to provide a professional, data-driven view of a curated wardrobe.

## Access Points

Users can access the Rotation and Playlist features through the following entry points:

- **Home Screen Quick Actions**:
    - **Calibration**: Triggers the Zero-Cloud facial scan to establish the user's color profile.
    - **Style Playlist**: Opens the 7-day style forecast and orchestration suite.
- **Wardrobe Landing (Curated Closet)**:
    - **Intelligence**: Deep dive into CPW and Chromatic Core analysis.
    - **Inventory**: Full list of garments with freshness indicators.
    - **Footprint**: Quantitative breakdown of category concentration.
    - **Behavior**: Usage distribution and "Wardrobe Heroes" ranking.

---

## 1. Curated Closet (The Hub) — *"What is the state of my wardrobe?"*
**Purpose**: The central analytics hub. It frames the wardrobe as a portfolio of style investments.
- **Key Metrics**: Glow Score (utilization), Diversity Index (distribution), Total Pieces, and Total Value.
- **Visuals**: Uses premium Serif typography and high-contrast gradients (Holographic vs. Forest Green).

## 2. Strategic Diversity (The Footprint) — *"Where is my wardrobe concentrated?"*
**Purpose**: Quantitative analysis of the wardrobe's architecture.
- **Key Metrics**: Percentage share of pieces and investment per category (Verticals).
- **Visuals**: Progress gauges showing category concentration and AI-driven "Pro Insights."

## 3. Usage Metrics (The Behavior) — *"What am I actually wearing?"*
**Purpose**: Behavioral analytics exposing rotation frequency.
- **Key Metrics**: Frequency distribution (Never to 20+ wears) and "Wardrobe Heroes" ranking.
- **Features**: Explicit tracking of "Resting" items (those currently in the 48-hour cooldown period).

## 4. Style Intelligence (The Analysis) — *"What does my wardrobe say about me?"*
**Purpose**: Advanced "Style DNA" view focusing on efficiency and color.
- **Key Metrics**: Cost Per Wear (CPW) and the "Chromatic Core" color signature.
- **Safety**: Unused items are labeled as **"NOT DEPLOYED"** to prevent division-by-zero and highlight unextracted value.
