## 2. Description of the Service

KoColor is a local-first personal wardrobe, cosmetic, and styling operating system designed for Android devices. The App provides the following core capabilities:

* **Digital Collection Archives:** Tools to catalog and manage apparel ("The Wardrobe") alongside cosmetics, skincare prep, grooming, and fragrance inventories ("The Vanity").


* **Deterministic Color Science:** Algorithmic color evaluation based on CIELAB (L*C*h°) color space geometry, relational color temperature, and personal appearance telemetry.


* **Environmental & Circadian Context:** Integration of local atmospheric telemetry—including temperature, humidity, wind speed, and UV index forecasts—to inform daily garment and skincare prep suitability.


* **AI-Assisted Synthesis & Scoring:** Generation of coordinated outfit and cosmetic recommendations ("Style Blueprints"), aesthetic rationales, and multi-axis evaluations ("FASHIONISTA Score").


* **Wardrobe Analytics:** Local tracking of wear frequency, rotation health metrics, color history timelines, and cost-per-wear insights.


* **Verified Starter Packs & Offline Operation:** Access to pre-compiled, cryptographically signed `.kpkg` capsule catalogs delivered via our content delivery network (`cdn.kocolor.com`), backed by an on-device deterministic fallback engine ("Local Architect") for offline functionality.

## 3. Privacy, Local-First Architecture & Data Practices

Your use of KoColor is governed by our local-first engineering principles:

* **On-Device Storage:** Your personal wardrobe inventory, cosmetic catalog, wear history, and appearance telemetry are stored locally on your device.


* **On-Device Facial & Biometric Processing:** When you use facial skin tone or appearance analysis features, image processing occurs strictly on your device. Raw facial photographs are never uploaded to KoColor servers or transmitted to cloud AI providers.


* **Zero-Knowledge Cloud Routing:** When cloud AI synthesis is invoked, the App transmits only text-based and numeric wardrobe/context telemetry authenticated via anonymous session tokens and hardware-verified Google Play Integrity / Firebase App Check attestation.


* **No Advertising or Health Tracking:** KoColor does not display third-party advertisements, does not collect or use Google Advertising ID (`AD_ID`), and does not access or collect Android Health Connect fitness or medical records.


* **Data Loss Responsibility:** Because personal inventory and wear data reside locally on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing local application storage may permanently erase your collection unless you maintain device-level backups.
