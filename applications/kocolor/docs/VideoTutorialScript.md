# KoColor Video Tutorial & Storyboard
**Title:** Master Your Style with KoColor
**Target Audience:** New users, fashion enthusiasts, and privacy-conscious digital organizers.
**Tone:** Elegant, intelligent, empowering, and seamless.
**Pacing:** Dynamic but clear. Emphasizing the transition between physical items and digital intelligence.

---

## 🎬 Scene 1: The Introduction
**Visual:**
*   Fast-paced montage: A messy physical closet, a chaotic makeup bag, transitioning smoothly into the clean, minimalist KoColor UI.
*   The text **"Your Personal Style Operating System"** fades in.
*   Camera pans across the Home Dashboard, showing real-time weather, UV index, and daily rituals.

**Voiceover (VO) / Host:**
> "Welcome to KoColor. It’s not just a digital closet; it’s a privacy-first, on-device operating system that unifies your wardrobe, cosmetics, and daily routines into one intelligent platform."

**On-Screen Action:**
*   User taps the KoColor app icon to launch.
*   A brief "On-Device AI" badge flashes, emphasizing privacy.

---

## 🎬 Scene 2: Environmental Awareness (The Home Dashboard)
**Visual:**
*   Screen recording of the **Home Tab**.
*   A subtle animation highlights the Weather widget (e.g., "75°F, UV Index 8, Sunny").
*   The UI automatically suggests a "High UV" sunscreen reminder in the Morning Ritual section.

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "Great style starts with your environment. Every morning, KoColor checks the local weather and UV index, instantly adapting your beauty rituals and outfit recommendations to keep you comfortable and protected."

**On-Screen Action:**
*   User taps a checkbox on "Apply SPF 50" in their Morning Routine.
*   A small checkmark animation plays with a satisfying haptic feedback sound.

---

## 🎬 Scene 3: Digitizing Your Life (Smart Camera & Color Science)
**Visual:**
*   Split screen: Left side shows a user holding a physical sweater and a lipstick. Right side shows the KoColor Smart Camera UI.
*   The camera scans the sweater. A scanning reticle locks onto the fabric.
*   **Pop-up UI:** "Extracting Color Signature..." The screen displays dominant hex codes, Delta-E math matching, and seasonal palette assignment (e.g., "Warm Autumn").

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "Adding to your collection is effortless. Just point your camera. Our advanced Color Science engine extracts the exact mathematical color signature, matches it to standardized color spaces, and categorizes it by seasonal palette."

**On-Screen Action:**
*   User scans the sweater. It saves instantly to the Wardrobe.
*   User scans the lipstick. It saves to the Vanity, prompting the user to enter a "Period After Opening" (PAO) date.

---

## 🎬 Scene 4: The AI Stylist (Daily Style Plan)
**Visual:**
*   The user navigates to the **Collection Hub** and taps the **AI Stylist** button (sparkle icon).
*   A prompt box appears: "What is your intent for today?"
*   User types: *"Important office presentation followed by a casual dinner."*
*   A loading animation showing "Gemini Nano processing on-device..."

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "Don't know what to wear? Ask your AI Stylist. Running securely on your device, it analyzes your inventory, today's weather, and your specific intent to craft the perfect look."

**On-Screen Action:**
*   The screen generates a **Daily Style Plan**: It pairs the recently scanned sweater with complimentary trousers and the exact lipstick tone to ensure color harmony.
*   The UI shows a "Color Harmony Score: 98%".

---

## 🎬 Scene 5: Analytics & Habit Building
**Visual:**
*   The user taps "Commit Outfit" for the day.
*   Transition to the **Wardrobe Footprint** analytics screen.
*   Graphs animate in, showing "Cost Per Use (CPU)" going down for the sweater.
*   A pie chart shows the user's dominant wardrobe colors.

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "As you wear your items, KoColor learns. Track your Cost Per Use, view your dominant color palettes, and receive alerts before your cosmetics expire. Build sustainable habits that save you money and reduce fashion fatigue."

**On-Screen Action:**
*   User scrolls through their Vanity. A red badge appears next to a moisturizer: "Expires in 5 days."
*   User adds it to their shopping cart.

---

## 🎬 Scene 6: Outro & Call to Action
**Visual:**
*   The user locks their phone and puts it in their pocket, stepping out the door looking confident in the outfit KoColor suggested.
*   Fade to the KoColor logo.
*   Text on screen: **"Download KoColor. Your Style. Your Data. On Your Device."**

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "Elevate your aesthetic, simplify your mornings, and keep your data completely private. Start your style journey with KoColor today."

---

## 🎥 Production Notes for the Director
*   **Transitions:** Use fluid, swipe-based transitions that mimic Jetpack Compose Navigation.
*   **Colors:** The video color grading should shift to match the "Seasonal Palette" being discussed (e.g., warm lighting during the Autumn sweater scan).
*   **Music:** Lofi/Chill-hop instrumental—steady, focused, and relaxing to mirror the app's goal of reducing decision fatigue.
*   **Key Emphasis:** Always visually emphasize that the AI and Data are *Local/On-Device*. Use padlock or device-chip icons during loading states.
