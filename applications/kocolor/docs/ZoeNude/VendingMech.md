## 1. How a purchase would work

I envision the following process:

1. Identify: Customer opens Zoe Nude and scans a QR code or taps an NFC point on the machine.
2. Discover: The app identifies the machine and retrieves its available products, pricing, and inventory from Zoe Nude's cloud.
3. Purchase: Customer selects a product and pays in the app.
4. Authorize: The cloud generates a secure, single-use authorization tied to that machine, product, and transaction.
5. Dispense: The phone communicates the authorization locally to the machine using NFC.
6. Report: The machine confirms dispensing to the phone, which reports the completed transaction to the cloud.
7. Update: Cloud inventory, sales, customer history, and analytics are updated.

The important detail: the machine does not need its own internet connection. The customer's phone provides the connection to the Zoe Nude cloud.
