## **My recommendation for Flow**

I would actually produce four separate clips rather than asking Flow to generate the entire sequence in one shot. This gives you much better control over the machine's appearance and the customer's actions.


**Clip 1 — Shop & Pay**

Show the Zoe Nude app and product selection.


**Clip 2 — Tap & Select**

Show NFC authorization and the mechanical button.


**Clip 3 — Pull**

Focus on the satisfying mechanical interaction.


**Clip 4 — Collect**

Show the product arriving and the customer enjoying it.

**One important production tip:** First generate a single approved image of the Zoe Nude dispenser. Then use that same image as the visual reference for all four Flow clips. This helps maintain the same machine, buttons, pull handle, materials and proportions throughout the advertisement.

For social media, I would use vertical 9:16 video, with a gentle, playful mechanical sound effect and elegant music.

The message we want people to remember is:

**Zoe Nude. A whole new way to shop for beauty.**
