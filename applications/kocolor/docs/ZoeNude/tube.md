## The material you're describing

It's commonly called bamboo bio-composite or bamboo-based bio-resin.

It typically combines:

- Bamboo powder or finely processed bamboo fibre.
- Plant-derived bio-resin (the binding polymer).
- Natural mineral additives or pigments, depending on formulation.

The resulting material can be molded into rigid cosmetic containers, caps, bottles and twist-up tubes.
