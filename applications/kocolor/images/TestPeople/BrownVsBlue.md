A single hexadecimal shift in the visual anchor triggers a massive divergence in the engine's harmonization logic. By swapping the eye color, you are fundamentally changing the **Contrast Level** and the **Temperature Balance** of the biological canvas.

Here is exactly how your `StyleSimulatorEngine` must mathematically branch when processing these two distinct profiles.

### 1. The Payload Shift (Data Extraction)

When the local device maps the 468 points of the face, the extraction math will generate two different RoomDB `FashionProfile` entities.

* **Left Subject (Brown Eyes):** The engine extracts an Eye Iris value of roughly `#5A3C26` (Warm Amber-Brown).
* **Right Subject (Blue Eyes):** The engine extracts an Eye Iris value of roughly `#5B7B88` (Cool Slate-Blue).

The skin highlight (`#E8C5A9`) and hair dominant (`#4E382A`) remain identical.

### 2. The Algorithmic Branching (Tier 1 & 1.5 Reasoning)

When these manifests are serialized and sent to the LLM (either Cloud Flash or Local Nano), the reasoning engine must apply entirely different color theory formulas.

**The Left Canvas: Monochromatic Harmony**
The left profile is deeply cohesive. The warm brown eyes match the warm undertones of the skin and hair. The engine calculates a **Low Color Contrast** ratio.

* **The Logic:** The AI will default to an "Analogous" or "Monochromatic" blending strategy. It does not need to balance competing temperatures. It will classify this strictly as a **Soft Autumn**.

**The Right Canvas: Split-Temperature Contrast**
The right profile introduces a physiological clash. The warm hair and skin are abruptly interrupted by a sharp, cool focal point. The engine calculates a **Medium-High Color Contrast** ratio.

* **The Logic:** The AI must switch to a "Complementary" styling strategy. It has to acknowledge the warm base but strategically deploy colors that bridge the gap to the cool eyes. It will likely shift the classification toward a **Soft Summer** or a **Bright Spring**, depending on the precise formality intent.

### 3. The Synthesized Output (Wardrobe & Makeup)

Because the engine's internal logic branches, the final JSON blueprint returning to your UI will look entirely different.
