# User Guide: Seaweed 🌊
**Mastering the Financial Awareness Engine**

Welcome to Seaweed. This guide will help you move from simply "tracking" your money to actively "controlling" it using our real-time intervention system.

---

## 1. Getting Started: AI Smart Capture
The easiest way to log transactions is through our **AI Vision Engine**.

1.  Tap the **Add (+)** button on the Transactions screen or the **Camera** icon in the Add Transaction form.
2.  Snap a clear photo of your receipt.
3.  **AI Analysis**: Seaweed will automatically extract the Merchant Name, Amount, and Date.
4.  **Categorization**: The engine will intelligently map the receipt to your existing categories (e.g., Coffee, Groceries).
5.  **Review & Save**: Verify the details and tap Save.

> **Tip**: You can add a "Context Comment" (e.g., "Lunch with the marketing team") to help the AI refine its description and category mapping.

---

## 2. Decision-Time Control: The Envelope System
Seaweed doesn't just show you where your money went; it controls where it *can* go.

### Setting Up Envelopes
1.  Navigate to **Settings > Spending Control**.
2.  Tap the **+** button to create a new **Envelope**.
3.  Give it a name (e.g., "Dining Out") and a **Monthly Limit**.
4.  **Link Categories**: Select which transaction categories belong to this envelope. For example, "Coffee," "Fast Food," and "Restaurants" might all be linked to the "Dining Out" envelope.

### Real-Time Intervention
When you try to save a transaction that exceeds an envelope limit:
*   **The Intervention Dialog**: Seaweed will intercept the save and show you an alert.
*   **Awareness Impact**: The system will show you the exact consequence—e.g., *"This reduces your potential savings for the Vacation Fund by $42."*
*   **The Choice**: You must consciously choose to **Approve Anyway** (Override) or **Cancel** the transaction.

---

## 3. The Dashboard: Understanding "Real Money"
The Home screen is designed to give you instant financial clarity.

*   **Real Money Hero**: Shows you exactly how much flexible spending money you have left *after* all your fixed costs (bills) and budget envelopes are subtracted from your income.
*   **Needs vs. Wants**: A visual breakdown of your spending importance. Remember: your "Wants" are where your behavioral change happens.
*   **Savings Rate**: Monitors if you are meeting the "20% Rule" for long-term wealth building.

---

## 4. Advanced Transaction Tools
For those who need more detail:

*   **Tip Widget**: Easily add standard percentages (15%, 18%, 20%) or custom tip amounts to your total.
*   **Bill Splitting**: Tap "Split Bill" to see how much each person owes. The transaction total remains yours, but the "per-person" calculation is always one tap away.
*   **Recurring Detection**: Mark transactions as recurring to let Seaweed automatically factor them into your "Fixed Costs" calculation.

---

## 5. Cash Flow Awareness
Monitor the **pace** of your financial life.

*   **Money In vs. Money Out**: View your net balance in real-time.
*   **Pace Projection**: Seaweed calculates if your current daily spending speed will lead to a surplus or a deficit by the end of the month.
*   **Awareness States**:
    *   🟢 **Positive**: You are on track to meet your savings goals.
    *   🟡 **Neutral**: You are living within your means.
    *   🔴 **Warning**: Your current pace exceeds your monthly income.

---

## 6. Privacy & Data Sovereignty
Seaweed is built with a **Zero-Footprint Mandate**.

*   **Local Storage**: All your data—transactions, envelopes, and receipt photos—is stored in a secure database **on your device**. 
*   **No Central Servers**: ZoeWave LLC has no access to your financial life.
*   **Data Deletion**: To wipe all data, simply uninstall the app or clear the app storage in Android Settings.
*   **Cloud AI**: If you use the AI features, data is processed ephemerally by Google Gemini and is never retained by us.
