# Privacy Policy for Seaweed

**Effective Date:** April 21, 2026

**ZoeWave LLC** ("we", "us", or "our") provides the Seaweed application as a Commercial service. This application is intended for use as is.

### 1. Information Collection and Use: The Zero-Footprint Mandate
Seaweed is engineered with a "Zero-Footprint" mandate.
* **Local Storage Only:** All data created within the app—including transactions, bills, categories, budget envelopes, and receipt images—is stored exclusively in a secure local database on your device.
* **No Central Servers:** We do not own, operate, or utilize central servers to store your financial data. We have no access to your personal information, and your data is never synchronized to external servers by ZoeWave LLC.
* **No Account Required:** You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the service.

### 2. Generative AI and Data Processing (BYOK Model)
Seaweed provides an interface for advanced "Financial Awareness" using Generative AI (Gemini Flash).
* **Bring Your Own Key:** AI intelligence is not a native component of the software. Users may optionally provide their own API Key generated via Google AI Studio.
* **Ephemeral Processing:** When using AI to analyze receipts, image data is transmitted directly to the service provider (Google) for processing. This data is handled in-memory and is not retained, viewed, or stored by ZoeWave LLC.
* **Liability Boundary:** The contract for the use of AI intelligence exists between the User and the API provider. ZoeWave LLC acts as the tool manufacturer (the "Vehicle") and is not responsible for the governance of the "Fuel" (the API Key) provided by the user.

### 3. Device Permissions
To provide core functionality, Seaweed requires the following permissions:
* **Camera:** Used solely to capture receipts for automated transaction and budget extraction.
* **Location:** Optionally used to tag transactions with a location for enhanced financial awareness and spatial spending history.
* **Storage:** Used to save your receipt photos locally on your device.
* **Play Age Signals:** Used to verify ecosystem status and ensure that high-fidelity Cloud AI features are only initialized for authorized adult accounts.

### 4. Advertising and Tracking
* **No Advertising ID:** Seaweed does not use the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) for tracking or profiling. This permission is explicitly removed from our application artifacts.
* **No Third-Party Ads:** We do not show advertisements within the app.

### **5. Third-Party Services and API Integration**
Seaweed utilizes a limited number of third-party services to ensure application stability and provide optional advanced features.

#### **A. Stability and Analytics (Standard)**
The app uses industry-standard SDKs to monitor performance and anonymized usage trends:
* [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/) (For stability and bug reporting)
* [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics) (For anonymized usage metrics)

#### **B. Payment Providers (Optional)**
Seaweed integrates with payment platforms (Stripe and Google Pay) to facilitate secure transactions. We do not store your credit card or sensitive financial credentials on our servers. All payment data is handled by the respective providers under their own policies.

#### **C. User-Provided Intelligence (Optional)**
To enable AI receipt extraction, users may optionally provide a **Google Gemini API Key**.
* **Local Storage:** Your API Key is stored exclusively in your device's secure local storage. 
* **Governing Policies:** Your interactions with Gemini are governed by the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 6. Children’s Privacy
Seaweed is a professional financial utility rated **13+**. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

### 7. Data Deletion and Security
Since all data resides on your device, you maintain absolute sovereignty over your information.
* **Data Deletion:** Deleting the Seaweed application instantly and permanently removes all local databases and stored images.
* **Security Recommendation:** We recommend utilizing device-level security (biometrics/passcodes) to protect your financial data.

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are encouraged to review this page for updates.
