# Store Listing: Seaweed 🌊

### App Name
**Seaweed: Financial Awareness Engine**

### Short Description
Transform your spending habits with real-time AI automation and behavioral "envelopes."

### Full Description
Take control of your financial future with **Seaweed**, the high-precision "Financial Awareness Engine" designed for intentional spending. Unlike traditional tracking apps that only look back at your history, Seaweed empowers you with **Decision-Time Control** to master your money the moment you reach for your wallet.

#### 🤖 AI-POWERED AUTOMATION
Stop manual entry. Seaweed uses advanced Multimodal AI to instantly extract merchant names, amounts, and dates from your receipts. Just take a photo, and let our engine handle the rest—automatically mapping transactions to your categories with surgical precision.

#### ✉️ SMART ENVELOPE MANAGEMENT
Go beyond simple budgeting. Seaweed uses behavioral "envelopes" to set real-time boundaries for your spending.
*   **Boundaries, Not Just Targets**: Group categories into envelopes like "Dining" or "Entertainment."
*   **Real-Time Enforcement**: Seaweed intercepts transactions before they happen. If you exceed your envelope limit, the system intervenes, helping you make a conscious choice between an override or a saving.
*   **Behavioral Tension**: Analyze the gap between your "Needs" and your "Wants" to find exactly where your spending change can happen.

#### 🔒 ZERO-FOOTPRINT PRIVACY
Your financial data belongs to you. Period.
*   **Local Storage**: Seaweed is built with a Zero-Footprint mandate. All your data stays on your device in a secure, local database.
*   **No Accounts Required**: No emails, no logins, no central servers.
*   **BYOK Intelligence**: You own the AI. Optionally provide your own Gemini API Key to enable high-fidelity extraction while maintaining absolute sovereignty over your data.

#### ✨ MODERN ANDROID EXCELLENCE
Experience a "Gold Standard" interface built with the latest Modern Android Development (MAD) principles.
*   **Fluid & Reactive**: 100% Jetpack Compose for a smooth, lag-free experience.
*   **Adaptive Layouts**: A premium experience that looks beautiful on phones, tablets, and foldables.
*   **Decision Dashboard**: A gorgeous home screen that shows you exactly how much "Real Money" you have left after your fixed costs and budget envelopes.
