# User Guide: Seaweed 🌊
**Mastering the Financial Awareness Engine**

Welcome to Seaweed. Most financial apps are "rearview mirrors"—they tell you what happened after the money is already gone. Seaweed is a **windshield**. This guide will help you move from simply "tracking" your money to actively "controlling" it using our real-time intervention system.

---

## 1. The Idea Behind Seaweed

The core philosophy of Seaweed is **Decision-Time Control**. 
When you spend money, you shouldn't just record it for later; you should understand the impact of that purchase *right now*.

Seaweed introduces **Transparent Friction**. If you are about to overspend, the app doesn't just quietly log the transaction. It intervenes, forcing you to consciously override a boundary or reconsider the purchase. It is the difference between a simple diet tracker and a personal trainer slapping the donut out of your hand.

---

## 2. Ways to Budget in Seaweed

Seaweed supports multiple layers of financial awareness, allowing you to choose how tightly you want to manage your money.

### Level 1: Categories (Descriptive Tracking)
*   **What it is:** Labels like "Coffee", "Groceries", or "Uber".
*   **How it works:** You assign categories to transactions to see where your money goes at the end of the month.
*   **Best for:** General awareness and analyzing historical spending habits.

### Level 2: Budgets (Quantitative Tracking)
*   **What it is:** Setting a target amount for a specific category (e.g., "$50/month for Coffee").
*   **How it works:** You can check your progress against this goal. If you exceed it, a chart turns red.
*   **Best for:** Goal setting and monitoring your performance without strict enforcement.

### Level 3: Envelopes (Behavioral Boundaries)
*   **What it is:** A strict spending limit applied across *multiple* categories (e.g., a "Dining Out" envelope containing Coffee, Fast Food, and Restaurants).
*   **How it works:** Envelopes are enforced at the moment of purchase. If a new transaction pushes the envelope over its limit, Seaweed throws an **Intervention Dialog** before the transaction is saved.
*   **Best for:** Real-time spending control and curbing impulse purchases.

## 3. Core Features & How to Use Them

### 📸 AI Smart Capture (Receipt Scanning)
Stop manual entry. Let the engine do the work.
1.  Tap the **Add (+)** button on the Transactions screen or the **Camera** icon in the Add Transaction form.
2.  Snap a clear photo of your receipt.
3.  **AI Analysis**: The Vision Engine extracts the Merchant Name, Amount, and Date.
4.  **Categorization**: The engine intelligently maps the receipt to your existing categories.
5.  **Review & Save**: Verify the details and tap Save.
*   *Tip: Add a "Context Comment" (e.g., "Lunch with the team") to help the AI refine its mapping.*

### ✉️ Spending Control (Envelopes)
Set up your behavioral boundaries.
1.  Navigate to **Settings > Spending Control**.
2.  Tap the **+** button to create a new **Envelope**.
3.  Give it a name (e.g., "Dining Out") and a **Monthly Limit**.
4.  **Link Categories**: Select which transaction categories belong to this envelope.
5.  **Intervention**: When you try to save a transaction that exceeds this limit, Seaweed will intercept it. You must consciously choose to **Approve Anyway** or **Cancel**.

### 🦸‍♂️ The Dashboard & "Real Money"
Instantly understand your financial reality on the Home screen.
*   **Real Money Hero**: This shows exactly how much flexible spending money you have left *after* subtracting all fixed bills and active budget envelopes from your income.
*   **Needs vs. Wants**: A visual breakdown of spending importance. Focus on the "Wants" ring—that is where behavioral change happens.
*   **Savings Rate**: Monitors if you are meeting the standard "20% Rule" for long-term wealth building.

### 🧮 Advanced Transaction Tools
Tools for complex scenarios.
*   **Tip Widget**: Easily add standard percentages (15%, 18%, 20%) or custom tip amounts to your total when adding a transaction.
*   **Bill Splitting**: Tap "Split Bill" to see how much each person owes. 
*   **Recurring Detection**: Mark transactions as recurring so Seaweed automatically factors them into your "Fixed Costs" calculation.

### ⏱️ Cash Flow Awareness
Monitor the *pace* of your spending.
*   **Money In vs. Money Out**: View your net balance in real-time.
*   **Pace Projection**: Calculates if your current daily spending speed will lead to a surplus or deficit by month-end.
*   **Awareness States**: 🟢 Positive (Savings track), 🟡 Neutral (Breaking even), 🔴 Warning (Exceeding income).

## 4. Privacy & Data Sovereignty
Seaweed is built with a strict **Zero-Footprint Mandate**.
*   **100% Local Storage**: All your data (transactions, envelopes, photos) is encrypted and stored locally on your device. 
*   **No Central Servers**: ZoeWave LLC has no access to your financial life.
*   **Data Deletion**: To wipe all data, simply uninstall the app.
*   **Cloud AI (BYOK)**: If you use the AI features, data is processed ephemerally by Google Gemini. You have the option to Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) for absolute control.
