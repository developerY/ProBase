# Seaweed Video Tutorial & Storyboard
**Purpose:** This document serves as the official script and screen direction guide for the Seaweed product video.

---

## Scene 1: The Hook & Introduction
**Screen Direction:** 
* Start with a blurred background of a busy coffee shop or a checkout counter. 
* Fade in the Seaweed App Icon in the center, which then transitions smoothly into the app's **Home Screen (Dashboard)**.
* Highlight the "Real Money Hero" number at the top of the screen. 

**Voiceover (Copy):**
> "Most budgeting apps are like rearview mirrors—they tell you where your money went after it’s already gone. Welcome to Seaweed. The app built to be your financial windshield. It doesn't just track your spending; it controls it."

---

## Scene 2: The Dashboard ("Real Money")
**Screen Direction:**
* The camera pans down the **Home Screen**. 
* Highlight the **"Needs vs. Wants"** rings. 
* Zoom in slightly on the **Savings Rate** card.
* A finger taps on the "Real Money" widget, briefly showing a tooltip or breakdown of (Income - Fixed Costs).

**Voiceover (Copy):**
> "Your dashboard gives you instant clarity. The 'Real Money' number isn't just your bank balance. It’s exactly what you have left to spend *after* your fixed bills and goals are paid. See your Needs versus Wants at a glance, because your 'Wants' are where you have the power to change."

---

## Scene 3: AI Smart Capture (Receipt Scanning)
**Screen Direction:**
* Cut to the **Transactions Screen**. 
* The user taps the floating **'+' (Add Transaction)** button.
* The screen shifts to the **Smart Camera** view. 
* The user snaps a photo of a physical receipt (e.g., a lunch receipt).
* An animated scan line sweeps over the image. The fields (Merchant Name, Amount, Date, Category) magically auto-fill on the screen.
* The user smiles (implied or shown) and taps **"Save"**.

**Voiceover (Copy):**
> "Logging transactions is effortless. With our AI Vision Engine, just snap a photo of your receipt. Seaweed instantly extracts the merchant, amount, and date, and automatically categorizes it for you. No more manual entry. Just snap, review, and save."

---

## Scene 4: Spending Control & The Intervention (Envelopes)
**Screen Direction:**
* Cut to the **Spending Control Screen**.
* The user creates a new Envelope called "Dining Out" and sets a $150 limit.
* Fast forward: Cut back to the **Add Transaction** screen. The user tries to manually log a $45 steak dinner.
* The user taps **Save**. 
* **Action:** The screen darkens, and the **Intervention Dialog** pops up abruptly with a warning icon.
* The screen zooms in on the text: *"This reduces your potential savings by $45 and exceeds your Dining Out envelope."*
* The user hovers over "Approve Anyway", but then taps **"Cancel Transaction"**.

**Voiceover (Copy):**
> "But here is where Seaweed changes the game: Decision-Time Control. By setting up behavioral 'Envelopes', you set strict boundaries. If a purchase pushes you over your limit, Seaweed intercepts it. This 'Transparent Friction' forces you to pause and choose: do you really need this, or would you rather save? It’s like having a personal financial trainer in your pocket."

---

## Scene 5: Cash Flow Pace & Analytics
**Screen Direction:**
* Cut to the **Cash Flow / Analytics Screen**.
* Show the **Pace Projection** graphic. It transitions from a Yellow (Neutral) state to a Green (Positive) state as the previous transaction was canceled.
* Scroll down to show the **Money In vs. Money Out** bar charts.

**Voiceover (Copy):**
> "Keep an eye on the pace of your financial life. Seaweed’s Cash Flow awareness tells you if your daily spending speed is putting you in the green or heading for the red, long before the month ends."

---

## Scene 6: Privacy & Outro
**Screen Direction:**
* The app UI shrinks back into the Seaweed App Icon.
* A padlock and a shield icon animate around the app icon.
* Text on screen: "100% Local. Zero-Footprint. Your Data is Yours."
* Fade to black with the App Store and Google Play badges.

**Voiceover (Copy):**
> "Best of all, Seaweed is built with a Zero-Footprint mandate. No central servers, no accounts, and 100% local encrypted storage. Your data is yours, period. Take control of your financial future today. Download Seaweed."
