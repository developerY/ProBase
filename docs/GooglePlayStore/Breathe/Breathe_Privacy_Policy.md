# Privacy Policy — Breathe

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 16, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Breathe**, an Android application published by **ZoeWave**.

Breathe is a simple breathing timer designed to help people relax and manage stressful moments through three steps:

1. **Relax (Breathe)** — Pause and focus on your breathing.
2. **Reflect** — Take a moment to reflect.
3. **Release** — Let go of tension and move forward.

Your privacy is important to us. Breathe is designed to work directly on your device without requiring an account, collecting personal information, or communicating with a developer-operated server.

This Privacy Policy explains how Breathe handles information when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect personal information through Breathe.**

The application does not require or provide:

- User registration or account creation.
- Login or authentication.
- A user profile.
- A personal information database.
- Personal identification or contact information.
- A connection to a ZoeWave backend or user database.

We do not collect your name, email address, telephone number, location, contacts, or other personal identifiers through the application.

We also do not collect or transmit your breathing sessions, timer activity, or personal usage information.

## 3. How the Application Works

Breathe is designed to operate locally on your Android device.

The breathing timer and its Relax, Reflect, and Release experience do not require personal information or a connection to our servers.

Any temporary application state required to operate the timer remains on your device. It is not transmitted to ZoeWave.

We do not maintain user accounts, personal profiles, or breathing-session records on external servers.

## 4. Information Sharing

**We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.**

Because Breathe does not collect personal information, there is no user information collected by us to sell or share with advertisers, marketing companies, or other third parties.

Breathe does not send personal information to ZoeWave or other third parties.

The distribution of Breathe through Google Play and the operation of the Android platform are subject to Google's and the device provider's own privacy policies. Those independent platform practices are outside our control.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Breathe does not maintain a remote database or cloud storage containing user information.

We do not retain personal information or breathing-session records on our servers.

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal information held by ZoeWave that requires deletion.

You can uninstall Breathe at any time. Any application data retained locally is subject to Android's device and application storage behavior.

## 6. Data Security

Breathe is designed to operate without transmitting personal information to ZoeWave.

Because we do not maintain a server-side user database or collect personal information through the application, we do not have a repository of user personal information to secure.

We encourage users to keep their Android operating system and devices up to date.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Breathe does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

The application does not require users to provide personal information to use its breathing timer.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Breathe does not depend on a ZoeWave-operated backend service to provide its core breathing timer functionality.

Google Play and Android may independently process information as part of their platform services. Such processing is governed by the applicable third-party privacy policies.

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the Breathe Android application and the privacy practices of ZoeWave.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its privacy practices change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised **Last Updated** date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.
