# Walkthrough - "Always Show Everything" Health Dashboard

I have updated the Health & Wellness dashboard to ensure all tracking capabilities are visible to you at all times, providing a clear map of the app's full potential even before your data is synced.

## Key Accomplishments

### 1. Persistent Dashboard Sections
- **Active Alerts**: The "Active Alerts" section is now permanent. When your vitals are stable, it displays a stylized **"All Systems Stable"** card, informing you that monitoring is active without the clutter of empty space.
- **Vitals Monitoring**: Integrated a persistent **Vitals section** that displays your heart rate. If no wearable is connected, it shows a subtle "Waiting for sync" state with a helpful prompt to connect your device.

### 2. Intentional "No Data" Styling
- **Visual Depth**: Sections with missing data now use a refined **`alpha(0.6f)`** transparency. This "grays out" the section to indicate inactivity while keeping the feature's visual footprint intact.
- **Micro-copy Improvements**: Replaced technical placeholders with user-friendly strings like "--- bpm" and "no data today," ensuring the dashboard feels like a living document ready to receive information.

### 3. Refined Activity Placeholders
- **Step & Calorie Cards**: Updated the Activity section to handle zero-states gracefully. When no movement is logged, the cards transition to a muted state with "no data" subtext, clearly distinguishing between a day of rest and a lack of sensor connection.

### 4. Cohesive Navigation Flow
- **Water Drop Gateway**: Maintained the high-fidelity **Water Drop visual** as a persistent summary. It continues to act as your primary gateway—tapping it will always take you into the spectacular, deep-dive Hydration page.
