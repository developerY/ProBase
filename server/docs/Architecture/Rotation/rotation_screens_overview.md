# KoColor Rotation Screens Overview

This document outlines the purpose and content of the four primary screens within the KoColor Rotation and Inventory system. These screens are designed to provide a professional, data-driven view of a user's curated wardrobe.

---

## 1. Curated Closet (Wardrobe Landing)
**Purpose**: The central analytics hub and entry point for wardrobe management. It provides an immediate "pulse check" on the user's style portfolio.

**Key Content**:
- **Glow Score**: A 0–100% metric representing wardrobe utilization (Unique items worn / Total items).
- **Diversity Index**: A qualitative rating (e.g., "Eclectic", "Strategic") measuring how evenly usage is distributed across categories.
- **Summary Stats**: High-level count of total pieces and the total financial investment of the portfolio.
- **Verticals Navigation**: Quick access to archived categories (Archive Verticals) for retrieval and inventory management.

## 2. Strategic Diversity
**Purpose**: Quantitative analysis of the wardrobe's architecture, helping users visualize where their investments are concentrated.

**Key Content**:
- **Portfolio Composition**: A detailed list of all categories with item counts and percentage bars relative to the total collection.
- **Pro Insights**: Contextual AI-generated summaries about the balance and diversity of the current collection.
- **Navigation**: Deep links into specific category covers for detailed inventory viewing.

## 3. Usage Metrics
**Purpose**: Analyzing the rotation frequency and "utility" of specific garments to identify "Wardrobe Heroes" and underutilized items.

**Key Content**:
- **Usage Distribution Chart**: A breakdown of the collection by wear frequency (Never, 1-5, 6-10, 11-20, 20+ wears).
- **Most Worn Pieces**: A ranked list of the top 5 most frequently logged garments, showing their relative usage against the top-performing item.
- **Glow Score Education**: Detailed explanation of the Glow Score logic and its impact on wardrobe ROI.

## 4. Style Intelligence (Wardrobe Analytics)
**Purpose**: A "Style DNA" view focusing on the financial performance and chromatic core of the wardrobe.

**Key Content**:
- **Portfolio Performance**: Key financial metrics including Total Value and Average Cost Per Wear (CPW).
- **Wardrobe Palette**: The "Chromatic Core"—a visual spectrum representing the dominant colors in the collection, allowing users to identify their preferred color signatures.
- **Style Efficiency**: A ranking of items by CPW, highlighting the most "efficient" investments in the closet.
